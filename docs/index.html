What you get

  • Speech recognition on your phone or computer with NVIDIA's Parakeet model, in 25 European languages.
  • On Android, a floating bubble appears when you tap a text field. Tap it, speak, and tap the tick.
  • On a computer, hold a shortcut key, speak, and let go.
  • A personal dictionary for names and words it should spell your way.
  • Updates itself. Optional sync between devices with a server you run yourself.
